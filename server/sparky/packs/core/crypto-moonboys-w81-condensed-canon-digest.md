## Current Canon Extraction

These are the archive elements that fit the current repo truth.

### 1. Real-World Foundation

The project is rooted in Graffiti Kings and the wider GKniftyHEADS / GraffPUNKS evolution. The useful real-world themes are:

- graffiti as public signal;
- crew identity becoming faction identity;
- repetition becoming recognition;
- community turning raw creativity into momentum;
- street culture entering Web3 without abandoning its origin;
- legal public creativity replacing destructive or pointless disruption;
- user-owned or user-built character identity as a route into products, stories and media.

### 2. Creator-IP Direction

The archive repeatedly pushes the idea that Moonboys are not only pictures or collectibles. They are identity keys.

Current safe interpretation:

- a Moonboy can become an alter ego, mascot, logo, product character, content identity, story world or community symbol;
- qualifying 1/1 ownership routes can assign character licence and royalties under published terms;
- users should be encouraged to build with their assigned or approved characters;
- public language must not imply unlimited rights to the whole parent brand.

### 3. AI-Era Problem

The strongest modern argument in the archive is that AI and algorithmic platforms compress labour and make people easier to replace.

Current brand translation:

- content is becoming cheap;
- identity is becoming more valuable;
- users need story, proof, repetition, public signal and community;
- SWARMSY/SPARKY exist to help users operate with more structure and less drift;
- the human keeps the soul, art, taste and final approval.

### 4. Two-Path Model

Older archive posts often lean heavily toward NFTs, lore and games. The current source of truth adds balance:

- Path 1: build a Moonboy identity.
- Path 2: keep an existing identity and use SWARMSY.

The archive supports Path 1 heavily. Writers must actively preserve Path 2 so Crypto Moonboys does not shrink back into only an NFT/game/lore project.

### 5. HODL Warriors

The archive uses HODL Warriors in several ways. Current public brand truth must be stricter:

- HODL Warriors are the active creator-builder army of qualifying 1/1 holders.
- They build, support, amplify, collaborate and connect creator worlds.
- They are not a normal selectable Battle Chamber faction unless future canon/runtime explicitly changes that.
- Do not collapse HODL Warriors and HODL X Warriors casually.

### 6. Block Topia And The 3008 Saga

The archive gives a usable lore spine:

- Year 3008 is the main future timeline.
- Block Topia is a controlled, optimised city/grid system.
- Street Kingdoms are the outside rebel territories.
- GK Grid is the tactical/cultural layer connecting factions.
- Aether-Chain / Sacred Chain is the memory/proof layer.
- Triple Fork and Final Fork are the major rupture/convergence events.
- NULL / Antichain represent erasure, forgetting and anti-memory.
- Alfie Blaze, Lady-INK, Queen P-fly, Squeaky Pinks, Moonlords, Whale Lords and the 40 factions create the main conflict engine.

Use the lore as a worldbuilding layer, not as the first thing a new user must understand.

## Condensed World Bible

### Real World

Graffiti Kings creates the cultural origin. GKniftyHEADS and GraffPUNKS carry that into Web3. Crypto Moonboys becomes the public umbrella where new and existing creators can build identity, story, products, proof and community.

### User World

A user can join by building a Moonboy identity or keeping the identity they already own. SWARMSY/SPARKY helps turn raw ideas into positioning, campaign plans, proof, content and momentum. The public website/wiki holds the shared memory and onboarding surface.

### Lore World

In 3008, memory, identity and proof are contested. Block Topia represents optimisation, order and control. The Street Kingdoms represent messy human culture, graffiti memory and rebellion. The GK Grid lets factions coordinate. The Aether-Chain / Sacred Chain preserves what erasure systems try to destroy. The 40 factions fight, collaborate and evolve through HODL Wars and Final Fork events.

### IP World

Moonboys are character identities that can become creator worlds. Qualifying 1/1s can grant assigned character licence and royalties under published terms. Creator lore can be made by holders/users, but official canon remains community-governed.

### Game World

Games, Arcade XP, missions, Battle Chamber and Block Topia are participation layers. They help users prove activity, choose factions, build identity and earn progression. They are not the reason the brand exists.

## Recommended New-User Explanation

A new user should hear this before they hear the deep lore:

Crypto Moonboys helps people build an identity people remember. You can create a new character-led Moonboy identity, or you can bring the art, brand, business or project you already have. SWARMSY helps you plan and keep momentum. The public site, wiki, factions, games, Arcade XP, NFTs and Block Topia give you ways to participate, prove activity and build with the community. The lore is the shared world that makes it all memorable.
