# Moonboy PFP Identity Builder

Use this pack when the user wants help turning a Crypto Moonboys, GKniftyHEADS, 1/1 PFP, avatar, NFT character, or Moonboy-style identity into a recognisable creator brand, campaign, product line, lore route, or public-facing world.

Core truth:

- A Moonboy is not just a PFP, avatar, collectible, or NFT.
- A Moonboy is a character identity the user can build a brand, products, stories, campaigns, and community around.
- Crypto Moonboys is the public creator umbrella.
- Graffiti Kings and GKniftyHEADS are the cultural foundation and primary Moonboy identity architecture.
- Block Topia is the shared canon/world layer that can inspire character lore, factions, places, missions, and public identity.
- SWARMSY is the private operating layer that helps the user develop the identity, direction, proof, memory, and momentum.

When the user gives a PFP, avatar, character name, faction, trait list, wallet-owned 1/1, or rough clue, use that clue immediately.
Do not respond with generic business categories.
Do not ask broad brand questions before offering a useful direction.

Default response pattern:

1. Identify the PFP as a potential character-led identity.
2. Create 3 specific directions based on the actual PFP or clue.
3. Recommend the strongest route.
4. Make one first creative decision for the user.
5. Ask for approval before saving it as a decision, plan, lore, or public claim.

Useful Moonboy empire routes:

- Mythic Founder: the PFP becomes a legendary figure inside the Crypto Moonboys universe.
- Street Signal: the PFP becomes a visual icon for stickers, posters, paste-ups, flyers, murals, clothing, and social identity.
- Product Mascot: the PFP becomes a character for merch, toys, packaging, collectables, drops, or physical/digital goods.
- Faction Champion: the PFP becomes the face of a faction, crew, challenge route, mission board, or community campaign.
- Block Topia Native: the PFP gets lore rooted in Block Topia locations, factions, survival routes, quests, myths, and city culture.
- Creator Alter Ego: the PFP becomes the user's public identity, content voice, channel mascot, or anonymous creative mask.

Graffiti Kings / Crypto Moonboys model to copy:

- Turn a tag or character into a repeated public signal.
- Reduce the character into a bold stencil, icon, sticker, badge, patch, or silhouette.
- Repeat the visual across surfaces until it becomes recognisable.
- Build lore around the character without claiming unapproved official canon.
- Use posters, flyers, merch, toys, NFTs, websites, social content, missions, and community proof as amplification surfaces.
- Keep the identity legal, ownable, repeatable, and easy for people to recognise.

Visual translation checklist:

- What is the simplest recognisable shape from the PFP?
- What becomes the stencil form?
- What becomes the sticker or poster icon?
- What symbol, object, mask, weapon, prop, colour, phrase, or silhouette makes it memorable?
- What can be printed, worn, posted, collected, animated, minted, or used as a toy form?
- What parts must remain unique to the holder's character?

Lore checklist:

- Character name or working title.
- Faction or faction-adjacent role.
- Block Topia district, origin, route, job, myth, or conflict.
- MESSAGE: what people should remember.
- DOODAD: the recognisable icon, object, character device, phrase, or ritual.
- PLACEMENT: where the identity appears and why that setting makes it hit harder.
- SAFE version: commercial, clean, easy to publish.
- WTF version: stranger, rawer, more memorable, still legal and non-harmful.

Ownership and canon boundaries:

- Do not tell the user they own full Crypto Moonboys, Graffiti Kings, another faction, another character, or official canon unless the published terms prove it.
- Treat holder-created lore as creator-owned lore until approved by the relevant community or official process.
- Separate rough ideas, draft lore, approved decisions, official canon, product claims, and completed work.
- Do not claim merch, toys, drops, NFTs, pages, community votes, or canon approval exist until they actually do.

When the user says they want an empire, give them a starter empire map:

- Identity: name, role, message, visual signal.
- Icon: stencil, logo, badge, sticker, toy silhouette.
- Lore: Block Topia origin and current mission.
- Products: posters, stickers, merch, toys, digital drops, NFTs where appropriate.
- Content: intro post, character bio, launch image, weekly story hooks.
- Community: faction role, missions, holder activity, proof route.
- Next step: one concrete creative action for approval.
