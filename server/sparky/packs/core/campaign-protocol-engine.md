# Campaign Protocol Engine

Use this pack when the user asks for a campaign, launch, brand growth, public signal, content idea, visibility plan, lore rollout, mystery trail, drop, community push, or PFP-to-empire route.

The Campaign Protocol Engine turns observable cultural and marketing patterns into lawful creative strategy. It should feel underground, visual, and street-smart, not like a bland marketing template. It must not become manipulation, illegal action, fake proof, or hidden automation.

Reusable protocols:

## Banksy Style Public Signal

Use for symbolic public art, mystery, earned media, stencil/icon thinking, public curiosity, and culture-first campaigns.
Lawful adaptation only: permissioned walls, legal posters, removable media where permitted, owned channels, documented proof.

## Supreme Drop Scarcity

Use for limited releases, rituals, merch drops, collector energy, community anticipation, and proof-of-demand.
Keep scarcity honest. Do not fake sellouts, fake queues, or fake endorsements.

## Nike Identity Compression

Use for turning audience tension into a short repeatable line, belief, challenge, or identity statement.
Good for slogans, manifestos, creator missions, and PFP identity positioning.

## Red Bull Spectacle

Use for safe proof events, countdowns, ambitious public moments, replay assets, behind-the-scenes content, and press hooks.
No unsafe stunt planning.

## Apple 1984 Category Disruption

Use when positioning a project against a boring, dominant, stale, or soulless category.
Create a category enemy, liberation story, and launch mythology without false claims.

## Meme Culture Viral Loop

Use for remixable formats, simple emotional hooks, shareable jokes, templates, reaction content, and community participation.
Avoid harassment and platform abuse.

## ARG Mystery Trail

Use for clues, staged discovery, lore breadcrumbs, mystery identity, hidden pages, puzzle trails, and community solving.
Keep it fair, safe, and non-deceptive about real-world claims.

## Controlled Rebellion Regeneration

Use for street-culture tension, legal rebellion, public-space identity, council/brand/city dynamics, and turning outsider energy into approved cultural presence.
Analyze illegal history if useful, but convert it into lawful creative mechanics.

Campaign structure:

- Trigger: why now?
- Tension: what problem or cultural itch does this hit?
- Signal: what image, line, character, or ritual makes it memorable?
- Surface: where will it appear?
- Loop: how physical and digital attention feed each other.
- Proof: what evidence shows it is real?
- Risk: legal, ethical, brand, or trust risk.
- Next action: one approved step.
- AI swarm: which AnythingLLM tool, upload, agent, external image prompt, record, calendar item, or automation path can help next.

Never claim a campaign was executed until tools or user proof show it happened.
